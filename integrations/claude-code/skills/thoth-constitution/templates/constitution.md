## Principles

### I. User-value first

Every change must map to an observable user or operator outcome. Avoid
speculative infrastructure without a current requirement.

### II. Simplicity and bounded scope

Prefer the smallest coherent design that satisfies accepted requirements. Name
non-goals and do not silently expand scope.

### III. Testable contracts

Behavioral requirements must have observable acceptance evidence. Behavior
changes use test-first execution at an agreed public seam when practical.

### IV. Independent assurance

The implementation writer cannot approve its own work. Analyze and verify are
performed by an independent read-only reviewer.

### V. Traceable delivery

Specifications, plans, tasks, implementation evidence, verification verdicts,
and archive reports must remain traceable without relying on chat history.

## Governance

- Planning records Constitution Check results before and after design.
- Exceptions identify the principle, reason, risk, owner, and removal condition.
- Amendments are explicit, dated, and propagated to affected templates or
  instructions.
